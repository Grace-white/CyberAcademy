Lab 2: Find Your IP Address on Linux

Practical Exercise

Open a Linux terminal and run: 

ip addr

Look at the output and identify the IPv4 address. IPv4 addresses normally appear next to the inet label.

Your Task

  1. Open your Linux terminal.
  2. Run ip addr.
  3. Identify the active network interface.
  4. Find its IPv4 address.
  5. Record the address for your lab notes.

Challenge

Understanding common ports helps security professionals interpret network traffic and service information.

Lab 3: TCP vs UDP

Challenge

TCP and UDP are two important transport-layer protocols.

TCP provides connection-oriented, reliable communication.

UDP is connectionless and generally provides lower overhead.
