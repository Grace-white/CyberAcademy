Routing and Switching

Learn how switches connect devices within networks and how routers connect different networks and direct network traffic.

Lesson 1: What Is Switching?

Switching is the process of forwarding data between devices within the same local network. A network switch connects multiple devices and forwards traffic to the appropriate destination.

Lesson 2: How a Switch Works

A switch learns the hardware addresses of connected devices and uses that information to forward traffic efficiently.

MAC Addresses

A MAC address is a hardware address associated with a network interface. Switches use MAC addresses to help forward frames within a local network.

Lesson 3: What Is Routing?

Routing is the process of determining how data should travel from one network to another. Routers use routing information to forward packets toward their destinations.

Lesson 4: The Role of a Router

Connecting Networks

A router can connect different networks and forward packets between them.

Choosing a Path

Routers use routing information to determine where packets should be forwarded next.

Lesson 5: Switching vs Routing

Switching primarily connects devices within a local network, while routing connects different networks and determines where packets should travel.

Switch

Connects devices within a local network and forwards traffic using information such as MAC addresses.

Router

Connects different networks and forwards packets using network-layer addressing and routing information.

Lesson 6: Why Routing and Switching Matter in Cybersecurity

Understanding routing and switching helps cybersecurity professionals understand network architecture, traffic paths, network segmentation, monitoring, and defensive security controls.

Key Takeaways

  • Switches connect devices within local networks.
  • Switches use MAC addresses to help forward traffic.
  • Routers connect different networks.
  • Routers use routing information to forward packets.
  • Routing and switching are fundamental networking skills.
  • Understanding network architecture is important for cybersecurity.
